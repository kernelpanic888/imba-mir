# IMBA / MIR v0.1.4

![IMBA / MIR — The Raven on the Green Road](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kernelpanic888/imba-mir/main/docs/assets/imba-mir-cover.png)

## [▶ PLAY IMBA / MIR](https://imba-mir-aleksey.kernelpanic888.chatgpt.site/)

Version `v0.1.4` opens Chapter II as a self-contained encounter and makes every
important transition visible, consequential, and formally grounded.

IMBA / MIR is both a **video game** and a **mathematics project**. Its living
World is executed through Lean 4; the interface is a visual projection of the
admitted state, never a substitute for it.

## Highlights

- Chapter II introduces three certified defense geometries: **Throw** reveals
  all four axes, **Anchor** stabilizes the strongest plane at a declared cost,
  and **Rift** commits to a hidden plane before its values are revealed.
- A fractured spell is no longer a dead end. Lean searches all `4⁴` formulae,
  proves a minimal reconstruction route, and exposes every replacement and its
  tension cost before the player confirms it.
- Every player input, formula transition, system decision, World response, and
  balance change appears in the Reality Slice.
- Initiative is explicitly a bounded capacity for preserving balance, never a
  source of damage.
- Every published chapter can be started independently; completion remains a
  personal chronicle marker.
- The spell book uses the available vertical space instead of compressing its
  most important construction states.
- English remains the default language, with Russian available from the menu.
- Owner-only aggregate launch, player, and action metrics are stored without a
  public counter or a player identity ledger.
